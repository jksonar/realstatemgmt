Maintenance Guidelines
Important Information
  • Provide as much detail as possible about the issue
  • Include photos when available to help diagnose the problem
  • Set the priority level appropriately based on urgency
  • Assign a vendor if you know who will handle the request
  • Schedule a date if maintenance has been planned
Priority Levels
  • Urgent Immediate attention required (safety hazards, major leaks)
  • High Should be addressed within 24-48 hours
  • Medium Should be addressed within 3-5 days
  • Low Can be scheduled during routine maintenance
Common Categories
  • Plumbing: Leaks, clogs, water pressure issues
  • Electrical: Outlets, lighting, electrical failures
  • HVAC: Heating, cooling, ventilation issues
  • Appliance: Refrigerator, stove, dishwasher repairs
  • Structural: Walls, ceilings, floors, windows
  • Exterior: Roof, siding, landscaping, driveway
  • Pest Control: Insect or rodent issues
